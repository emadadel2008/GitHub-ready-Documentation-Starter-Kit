# Architecture Decision Log

> This is an index for finding decisions, not a substitute for the rationale in an ADR when the context and trade-offs matter.

| ID | Date | Decision | Status | Owner | Context / requirement | ADR or evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `<ADR-001 or LOG-001>` | `<YYYY-MM-DD>` | `<short summary>` | `<Proposed / Accepted / Rejected / Superseded>` | `<name or team>` | `<link or requirement ID>` | `<link>` |

## Usage

- Keep entries brief and link to a full ADR for consequential decisions.
- Preserve the history when a decision is superseded; add a new entry and link both records.
- Use stable IDs and dates so other artifacts can reference a decision.
- Do not mark a proposal as accepted until the authorized decision owner confirms it.
