# Technical Risk Register

> Track significant cross-cutting technical, delivery, security, and operational risks in one owned register. Keep detailed assumptions in their source documents and link them here when they need ongoing management.

## Scoring Guidance

Agree on likelihood and impact scales with the team and use them consistently. The labels below are examples, not a prescribed scoring standard:

- **Likelihood:** `<Rare / Unlikely / Possible / Likely / Almost certain>`
- **Impact:** `<Low / Moderate / High / Critical>`
- **Status:** `<Open / Treating / Accepted / Closed>`
